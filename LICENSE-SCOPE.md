# Scope of the licence

`LICENSE` (MIT) covers the **Python source code in `tools/`**, written for this
publication.

The **hardware design files** — the Altium project, schematic, PCB and footprint
library documents under `hardware/`, and the export in `docs/` — are published by
Ali Shavakhi for reference and portfolio review. They are his own design work.
They are not covered by the MIT grant: no right is given to manufacture, sell or
redistribute boards derived from them, and no warranty of fitness or safety is
made.

If you want to reuse part of the design (a circuit block, a footprint, a layout
fragment), ask first: open an issue in this repository.

**Safety.** These boards are not certified. The design includes mains-referenced
circuitry or off-line power conversion; anything built from these files must be
reviewed, re-checked against current standards and tested by a competent engineer
before it is connected to anything that matters.
